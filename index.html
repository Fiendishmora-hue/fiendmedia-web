Fiendmedia

Fiendmedia is a private tool that prepares short Czech news videos for the YouTube channels Verum | Zprávy and Verum | AI.

It signs in with Google only to read the statistics of those two channels for their owner: how many people watched each video and for how long. It does not upload, edit or delete anything on YouTube, and it is not offered to other users.
