Fiendmedia – Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Who this applies to

Fiendmedia is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channels Verum | Zprávy and Verum | AI. It is not offered to other users.

What Google data it accesses

With the channel owner's consent, Fiendmedia has read-only access to:

  • basic information about the owner's own YouTube channels and videos (scope youtube.readonly);
  • YouTube Analytics reports for those channels, such as views, watch time and audience retention (scope yt-analytics.readonly).

It cannot upload, change or delete anything on YouTube.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner how their videos perform, so they can decide what to make next.

Where it is stored

The data and the Google access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and in the owner's private backups. Fiendmedia has no server of its own.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with anyone and not used for advertising. YouTube Analytics data is never sent to AI services. Only the titles and public view counts of the channel's own videos may be included in a request to an AI language model that helps choose topics for new videos. Fiendmedia does not use Google user data to develop, improve or train AI models.

Fiendmedia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The locally stored data can be deleted on request.
